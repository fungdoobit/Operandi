NOVA: three original directions

Same trope (a living shadow with blank white eyes in purple magic gas), three different silhouettes. Each is shown big and at roaming size on the battle and lesson backgrounds.

A · Comet

A round shadow head whose back streams into a comet tail of shadow flame, shedding sparks. Always looks like he's mid-flight.

B · Eclipse

A shadow teardrop with a glowing solar-eclipse corona behind his head. His tail tip crumbles into drifting dust.

C · Void hood

A tall shadow hood whose tip curls like a crescent moon. Inside is pure void; the hem breaks into shards that float away.
