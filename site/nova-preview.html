NOVA: hi-bit pixel art, three shapes

Same renderer as GOLEM: a 3D shadow model turned into dithered pixel art. Each is shown big on the battle background, then at roaming size on both backgrounds.

A · Flame-horned shadow

A shadow head crowned with four flame horns burning purple at the tips. His body thins into rising smoke.

B · Eclipse entity

A shadow teardrop in front of a burning eclipse corona. His tail sways and crumbles into dust.

C · Cosmic wraith

A hooded shadow with a void face and a ragged cape, shoulder spikes, and obsidian shards in orbit. More "boss you tamed".
