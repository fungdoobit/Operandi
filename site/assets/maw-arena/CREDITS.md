# Maw arena art

`mountains.png` and `foreground.png` are layers of the "Castle Road" pixel-art background (368x208, with sky, mountains and foreground layers) that Isaac supplied on 2026-10-24. The zip had no licence or author file; Isaac is responsible for its terms. The arena (`js/pixel/biome-scenes/maw.js`) mixes them with the procedural Overgrown Ruins scene.

## Plains (the Maw's current arena)

`plains/back.png`, `plains/middle.png`, `plains/front.png` are the layers of **Plains** by **Ansimuz** (ansimuz.com, "Battle Backgrounds" pack). Licence from the pack's readme: "You may use these assets in personal or commercial projects. You may modify these assets to suit your needs. Credit is not required but appreciated." The arena (`js/pixel/biome-scenes/dusk.js`) recolours the layers for the time of day and adds the sun, moon, stars, fireflies and birds. Credit Ansimuz in the game's credits.
